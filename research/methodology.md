# Survey Methodology

## Scope

20+ ransomware detection research papers reviewed across three detection paradigms.  
Publication range: 2018–2024.  
Sources: IEEE Access, IEEE conference proceedings, ACM Digital Library, Springer.

---

## Selection Criteria

Papers were included if they met all three of the following:

- Addressed detection methodology directly — not purely forensic or recovery-focused
- Included empirical evaluation against a ransomware dataset
- Covered at least one of the three core detection paradigms: signature-based, behavioral, or machine learning

---

## Analytical Framework

Papers were categorised by primary detection approach. Where a study combined approaches, it was categorised under its primary method and noted as hybrid-adjacent.

Each paper was evaluated against three questions:

1. What does this approach detect reliably?
2. Where does it fail structurally, and why?
3. What operational constraints did the authors note or omit?

---

## How Conclusions Were Drawn

Findings were not drawn from any single paper. Conclusions were based on patterns that appeared consistently across multiple studies — findings that held regardless of which specific dataset, implementation, or research group was involved.

Where individual papers reported contradictory results, the methodology examined whether the difference was explained by dataset composition, feature selection, or evaluation design rather than treating the contradiction as a true finding.
